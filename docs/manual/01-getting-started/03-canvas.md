# Canvas, zoom and the status bar

The canvas works the same in every app.

## Moving around

| Do this                      | To                            |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Scroll the mouse wheel       | zoom in or out at the pointer |
| Drag with the middle button  | pan                           |
| Hold Space and drag          | pan                           |
| Shift + wheel                | pan sideways                  |
| **F**, or the **Fit** button | fit the content in view       |
| **+** / **−**                | zoom in or out                |

The zoom level is shown on the canvas or in the status bar.

## Grid and rulers

- **#** or the **Grid** checkbox turns the grid on and off. Every fourth (level designer) or eighth (pixel editor) line is stronger.
- The **rulers** along the top and left count cells. A label marks the line after that many cells ("8" is the line after the 8th cell), and the cell under the pointer is shaded on both rulers, so you can count along them. In the level designer they count the active layer's cells, which can be bigger than the level's.

## The status bar

The bar at the bottom shows where the pointer is (pixel, cell or sprite), the active layer or value, the zoom, and where the project is saved ("browser library", a file name, or "not saved yet", with **•** for unsaved changes). Messages, such as what was exported or why something can't be done, appear on its right and fade after a few seconds.

## Panels and fields

- Number fields apply when you press **Enter** or leave them; Enter also gives the keyboard back to the shortcuts.
- Letter shortcuts are ignored while you type in a field, but **Ctrl+S**, **Ctrl+Shift+S**, **Ctrl+O** and **Ctrl+E** work everywhere.
- Buttons and lists can be reached with **Tab**; the focused one is outlined.
