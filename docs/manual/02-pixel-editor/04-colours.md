# Colours and palettes

## The current colour

The swatch under **Color** shows the colour the pen, shapes and bucket paint with. Click it to open the colour picker, where you choose a colour and its transparency. The colour picker tool (I, or hold Alt) takes a colour from the canvas.

## The palette

The palette below the swatch is a set of colours saved with the sprite.

- **Click** a colour to use it.
- **+** adds the current colour.
- **Right-click** a colour, or focus it and press **Delete**, to remove it.
- **Import** reads a palette file: `.hex` (one colour per line, as Lospec offers them) or `.gpl` (GIMP, Aseprite and Krita palettes).
- **Export** saves the palette as `.hex` or `.gpl`, so you can use it in other tools or sprites.

[Lospec's palette list](https://lospec.com/palette-list) is a good source of ready-made pixel-art palettes.
